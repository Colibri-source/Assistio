Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 28, 2026

Assistio collects and uses limited information needed to respond to inquiries, onboard clients, deliver automation services, and keep our systems secure. We do not sell personal data.

1. Information we collect

We may collect names, email addresses, company information, booking details, setup requirements, support messages, and limited technical data used for security and performance.

2. How we use information

We use information to answer inquiries, configure your agreed setup, coordinate launch, provide support, process billing, and improve reliability across the service.

3. Sharing

We only share information when required to deliver the service, such as with infrastructure, booking, communication, payment, or analytics providers that support Assistio operations.

4. Retention

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for service delivery, support, legal compliance, and legitimate business operations.

5. Your choices

You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your information where applicable. If a request affects active service delivery, we may explain the operational impact before action is taken.

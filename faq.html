FAQ

Short answers only—because most people don’t read long pages.

What exactly do you deliver in the first 60 days?
Website chat + Email + WhatsApp intake, routing rules, acknowledgements, human handoff, and basic reliability (logging + retries).
Do you provide “AI agent” features?
No. The MVP is about stable routing and handoff—not autonomous agents.
What do you need from me to start?
Access to your channels (email inbox, website, WhatsApp Business details), plus a short call to confirm routing rules.
How fast can we go live?
After onboarding and access are approved. Timing depends mostly on channel approvals—especially WhatsApp.
Is the website chat widget custom?
It’s a clean MVP widget designed to capture and route messages reliably. We keep it minimal by design.
Can you route messages to multiple people or teams?
Yes—basic routing rules are included (for example by topic, channel, or business hours).
What happens when automation isn’t enough?
We hand off to a human (your team) with clear context so replies stay fast and accurate.
How do payments work on a static website?
Checkout is handled by Stripe Payment Links. You pay securely, then you’ll be redirected and emailed a receipt.
Do you store customer data?
We only keep what’s needed to operate the MVP (message metadata and logs). See the Privacy page for details.
Do you support refunds?
Terms depend on work started and approvals. Please review the Terms page or contact us for clarification before purchase.
Can I cancel the monthly plan?
Yes. You can cancel anytime through Stripe or by contacting support.
Can I submit a question that isn’t listed here?
Yes—use the question form below and we’ll reply by email.

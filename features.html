Features

What you get in the first 2 months with Assistio.

This page only describes what we actually deliver in the first 1–2 months of the MVP: one AI assistant connected to your main channels, with real flows for FAQs, leads, and booking requests – nothing more, nothing less.

View pricing Talk about your use case

Engage – One assistant across your main channels

Customers don’t care where they message you; they just want fast, clear answers. In the MVP we focus on a single assistant that responds consistently across:

Website chat widget

A simple, modern chat widget you embed on your site. Visitors can ask questions, request prices, and start booking flows directly from your pages.

WhatsApp

Connect your WhatsApp number so Assistio can reply to common questions and route important conversations to your team.

Email

Repetitive support and inquiry emails can be answered by AI first. More complex cases are forwarded to a human with context.

Instagram & Facebook messaging

Handle DMs from Instagram and Facebook with the same assistant and the same rules, instead of answering everything manually.

Human handoff when needed

When the AI is unsure, or the customer asks for a person, we stop guessing and escalate to your chosen human channel (for example email or WhatsApp).

Automate – Flows we configure for you

Behind each conversation we configure no-code flows (using our automation backbone) so the assistant doesn’t just answer, it also moves work forward. In the first 2 months, we focus on:

AI answers from your FAQs & content

We load your FAQs, policies, and key information so the assistant can answer the most common questions in your tone and within your rules.

Lead capture

We set up simple flows to collect name, contact details, and a few qualification questions, so serious inquiries never get lost.

Booking & appointment requests

Customers can ask to book a consultation, visit, or service. The assistant collects the details and either sends them to your team or forwards them to your existing booking link/tool.

Internal notifications

When a high-value lead or complex case appears, we notify your team (for example by email or WhatsApp) with the full conversation summary.

Separate flows per client

If you are an agency, each client has its own flows, FAQs, and rules, so their assistant behaves like part of their brand.

Manage – Simple visibility & control

In the MVP we keep management simple on purpose. You get enough visibility to trust what the AI is doing, without a complicated dashboard.

Conversation history

We keep a basic history of conversations so you can review what the AI answered, what was escalated, and where to improve your FAQs.

Lightweight reporting

Simple overviews of volumes and escalations (for example per channel and per client), so you can see the impact without extra complexity.

Data per client workspace

Each client’s data, content, and flows are separated at the workspace level. This is especially important for agencies running multiple businesses.

How we implement this in the first 60 days

  1. Kickoff call. We understand your business, main use cases, and priority channels.
  2. Content & FAQ handover. You share your FAQs, website links, and any existing documentation.
  3. Setup & configuration. We connect your channels, configure AI answers and flows, and test everything internally.
  4. Pilot & adjust. We run a short pilot with real conversations, refine responses, and then fully turn it on.

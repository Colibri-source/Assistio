Be available on the channels your customers already use

Website chat widget

A simple, modern chat widget on your site where visitors can ask questions, see instant answers, and start lead/booking flows.

WhatsApp

Connect your WhatsApp number so Assistio can handle repetitive questions and pass important conversations to your team with context.

Email

What we actually build for you in the first 2 months

We keep the first version focused and realistic. In the MVP, we do not promise advanced analytics, deep CRM integrations, or complicated custom dashboards. Instead, we make sure the basics are strong and live:

AI answers from your FAQs

You share your top questions and answers, policies, and important pages. We configure the assistant so it responds using your information and tone, within your rules.

Lead capture flows

We set up simple flows to capture name, contact details, and key qualification questions. Serious leads are sent to your team by email or another channel you prefer.

Appointment & booking requests

The assistant collects booking requests (date, time, service, preferences) and either forwards them to your staff or sends customers to your existing booking link.

Internal notifications

When a high-value lead or sensitive case appears, we notify your team with a short summary and conversation context so they can respond quickly.

Conversation history

We keep basic logs of conversations so you can review what AI said, what was escalated, and what needs better FAQs or different rules.

Per-client workspaces for agencies

If you are an agency, each client runs in its own workspace with separated data, content, and flows. You manage everything under one roof, while staying organized.

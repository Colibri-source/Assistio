Frequently asked questions

What's included in the 14-day trial?

Full access to core features including knowledge base setup, routing, and booking. Trial includes up to 500 messages. We configure everything during onboarding and optimize throughout the trial.

Which channels are available?

Website chat is immediate. WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook require Meta approval (timelines vary). Email is available. We confirm activation options during onboarding.

How does booking work?

System qualifies leads through questions, then directs to your Google Calendar link. Complex scenarios route to your team with full context.

How do you ensure quality?

Structured knowledge base, pre-approved templates, confidence scoring, and automatic human escalation when AI is uncertain.

Can this work for healthcare?

Yes. Configured for general inquiries and booking only—no medical advice. Minimizes sensitive data collection. Compliance remains your responsibility per jurisdiction.

What about WhatsApp fees?

Meta charges per-message fees (vary by type/country), billed separately. We provide transparent estimates during onboarding based on your usage.

What do you need from us?

Services, pricing, FAQs, policies, brand voice examples, hours, team assignments, and booking calendar link.

How long is setup?

Typically few days after receiving content and credentials. Meta approvals (if needed) add time. Clear timeline provided during onboarding.

Multiple languages supported?

Yes—English, Romanian, Arabic. Start with one, add others later. Each requires configuration time for quality.

What security measures?

Industry-standard practices: access controls, minimum privileges, secure hosting. No specific certifications claimed at MVP stage.

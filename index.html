Features

Designed for booking-heavy operations and high message volume—without chaotic support.

AI agent with guardrails

Controlled, consistent replies—built around your policies and tone.

  • Knowledge base (FAQs, services, pricing)
  • Confidence rules + escalation to a human
  • No risky “hallucination” positioning

Booking-first flow

Qualification → scheduling → confirmation, with minimal friction.

  • Collect the right details for the booking
  • Route to Google Calendar link (MVP)
  • Fallback: handoff to your team

Multi-channel intake

Handle inbound messages where customers already are.

  • Website chat widget
  • Instagram/Facebook messaging (Meta approval)
  • WhatsApp + Email (availability varies)

Routing & handoff

The right message reaches the right owner, fast.

  • Routing by topic, urgency, language, or hours
  • Owners per category (sales/support/etc.)
  • Escalation when a human should take over

Operational intelligence

Reduce noise and surface what matters for the business.

  • Common intents + top questions
  • Lead quality signals (basic)
  • Iteration loop: improve scripts weekly

Safer by design

Built to avoid compliance trouble and overpromising.

  • Conservative claims and disclaimers
  • Clinic-friendly: inquiry handling, not medical advice
  • Data minimization prompts
Appointment booking
Collect the right details, confirm intent, and route to a booking link or your team—without breaking your flow.
